## About Coordinates Tracking

**Coordinates Tracking** is a lightweight World of Warcraft addon that displays your current map coordinates on-screen and on the World Map.

It is designed for players who want clear, configurable coordinate information for exploration, questing, gathering, farming, travelling, and following map-based guides.

## Features

### On-Screen Coordinates

Coordinates Tracking provides a movable on-screen coordinate frame that shows your current position.

*   Drag the frame to any position outside combat.
*   Right-click the frame to open the options panel.
*   Reset the frame position from the options panel if it is moved off-screen.
*   Optionally show your current zone name alongside the coordinates.
*   Show the zone name in a tooltip when the zone-name display is disabled.

### World Map Coordinates

The World Map can display:

*   Your current player coordinates.
*   The coordinates beneath the mouse cursor.

This makes it easier to follow coordinate-based guides, locate points of interest, and share map locations with other players.

### LibDataBroker Support

Coordinates Tracking supports LibDataBroker (LDB).

If you use a compatible LDB display addon, you can show coordinate information in its panel and disable the on-screen coordinate frame for a less cluttered interface.

### Appearance and Precision

Customize the coordinate display to suit your interface:

*   Font face, size, color, and outline.
*   On-screen frame scale, background, and transparency.
*   World Map coordinate font settings.
*   Tooltip scale and transparency.
*   Number of decimal places shown for coordinates.

## Why Coordinates Tracking?

Coordinates Tracking focuses on providing essential coordinate information without adding waypoint navigation, map markers, or unnecessary interface complexity.

It is a straightforward option for players who want to see their position, check World Map cursor coordinates, and customize how that information appears.

*   Your WoW client version and game branch.
